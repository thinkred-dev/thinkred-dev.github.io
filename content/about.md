**Think Red** is where I publish red team tradecraft: concepts, lab research, engagement craft, and the tools I build. Every offensive technique comes with the defender's side, covering how to detect it and how to mitigate it.

> *Think like an adversary. Write like a defender.*

## Focus areas
- Adversary emulation and threat-informed red teaming
- Active Directory and identity attack paths
- Red team infrastructure and OPSEC
- AI and LLM red teaming
- Detection engineering from the attacker's point of view

## How I work
- All research is performed in **environments I own or am explicitly authorized to test**.
- Nothing here comes from client engagements unless it has been generalized and approved.
- Techniques are mapped to [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/) and paired with detection and mitigation guidance.
